    Do you work with my insurance company?

    Yes. We work with all insurance providers, and we handle the documentation and the calls with your adjuster.

    The Evans office is open 24 hours a day, every day. For an emergency, call. A message through the form gets a reply within one business day.

    Do you rebuild, or only clean up?

    Both. Southeast Restoration is a licensed general contractor, so one company handles the cleanup, the repairs and full reconstruction.

    Is the work guaranteed?

    Yes. Our work comes with warranties, and the company is fully insured.

    Do you take commercial jobs?

    Yes: offices and retail, churches, schools, healthcare and senior care buildings, and industrial sites.
